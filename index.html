From Bolivia to Wyoming to Arkansas, each phase of my life added a new dimension to how I think, create, and deliver results.

May 2026 to Present
Slim Chickens
Marketing Technology Intern
At Slim Chickens, I work as a Marketing Technology Intern on the Digital Revenue and Guest Loyalty team, supporting the systems behind the Slimthusiast loyalty app and the broader guest experience.
My work lives at the intersection of marketing, customer support, and technology. I use platforms like Zendesk, Punchh, and SMG to resolve guest issues, support loyalty-app workflows, analyze recurring customer problems, and understand how digital systems shape the way guests experience the brand.
Beyond support operations, I have also been involved in Slim Chickens' content creation process, helping record and participate in social media videos while learning how a national restaurant brand builds content from the inside.
One of the biggest projects I am developing is an AI-assisted customer support workflow. The goal is to explore how AI can help classify support tickets, identify issue types, draft responses, and make the guest-support process faster and more consistent while still keeping a human in control.
This internship has shown me that marketing is not only campaigns and creative ideas. It is also systems, loyalty, customer experience, data, content, and the operational details that determine how people feel when they interact with a brand.

2025 to Present
Elite Level Scouting
Co-Founder & Marketing Lead
Elite Level connects Latin American soccer players with U.S. college programs. I built the brand and marketing strategy from scratch as an entrepreneurial project developed during university breaks in Bolivia.
I grew up playing fútbol in Bolivia. When I arrived in the U.S. on a soccer scholarship, I saw how isolating and confusing the recruiting process was. When I met Hugo, another Bolivian who had walked the same road, we built something for the families we left behind.
My role was everything that made the brand real. The content strategy. The visual identity. The storytelling. I knew what it felt like to be a family going through this without truly understanding what was happening. So I made it my mission to change that. Not through ads. Through story.
We built the content engine from zero. 677K+ views. 5+ families with scholarship offers. 10+ families currently in the process across South America.
I didn't just want to be part of this story. I wanted to be the one who told it right.
